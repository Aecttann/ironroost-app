## Full description (max 4000)
Enemy tanks roll in from the top of the map. Your base sits at the bottom behind a few rows of brick. Hold the line.

Ironroost is an original arcade tank game in the spirit of the 1980s consoles: a small board, walls you can shoot apart, and a steady stream of enemies that all want the same thing.

35 HAND-BUILT STAGES
Every map was made for this game. Blast through brick a quarter at a time, hide in the forest, slide across ice, and find a way around water and steel. Stages unlock one by one and get harder as you go.

KNOW YOUR ENEMY
Basic, fast, power and armored tanks each fight differently. Flashing tanks drop a bonus when you destroy them:
• Star – a stronger gun, one step at a time, up to shells that break steel
• Gun – full firepower at once
• Helmet – a shield for a few seconds
• Clock – every enemy freezes
• Shovel – your base walls turn to steel for a while
• Grenade – clears every enemy on the board
• Tank – an extra life
• Boat – drive across water

ENDLESS MODE
One arena, waves that keep getting tougher. After each cleared wave you pick one of three upgrades: rapid fire, twin shot, armor, ricochet, faster treads, piercing shells, a bulwark that turns your base walls to steel every wave, or salvage for an extra life. Every run builds a different tank. How far can you get?

COME BACK TOMORROW
Claim a daily reward for bonus lives on your next run. Keep the streak for seven days and earn a collection card. A streak freeze covers one missed day, so one busy day does not cost you the streak.

COLLECTION AND RECORDS
20 collection cards for kills, bonuses, campaign milestones and feats. Personal top-ten boards for the campaign and for endless, saved on your device.

PLAY YOUR WAY
• On-screen joystick and fire button, or a connected keyboard
• Local co-op: two players share one connected keyboard
• Portrait or landscape
• The game pauses itself when you leave the app
• English, Ukrainian, German, Hindi, Japanese, Korean, Turkish and Chinese

FREE, WITH OPTIONAL REWARDS
Ironroost is free to play. A small banner shows in the menus, never during a battle. Watching an ad earns two rewards, and only when you ask for them: one more life when your last one is gone, carrying on from where you fell, and a streak freeze on the daily reward screen, one every 48 hours. Ads never start on their own. No in-app purchases, no account, no sign-up.
